01 / About me

A little background.

I’m Dhyey, a first-generation immigrant from India and a high school senior in New Jersey. A lot of my work happens behind a camera or backstage.

My work in live event production includes audio engineering, lighting, and visuals. I also plan weekly assemblies for 40 children. Both roles involve coordinating with a team and taking responsibility for what happens during a program.

I take photographs and edit photos and video using Photoshop, Canva, Lightroom, DaVinci Resolve, and Premiere Pro. I’m also proficient with most Adobe Creative Cloud apps, all Microsoft applications, Windows, and other operating systems. I speak 3 languages. Alongside that work, I’m completing EMT training and planning to study biology in college.

05 / Growing up

Before all of this.

I’m a first-generation immigrant from India. My family moved to the United States to find better medical options for me. Since early childhood, I’ve lived with a systemic inflammatory disease involving an overactive immune system. My complications have included a life-threatening inflammatory crisis and interstitial lung disease. I still receive treatment.

My mom’s childhood blog also records six-year-old me spotting Windows 10 at Costco and asking my dad why he still used Windows 8. That interest in technology has stuck. Today, I put it to work through editing and audio engineering while working toward a future in medicine.
